The official upstream source was obtained independently, not from the withdrawn
Core/Har repositories or the separate HarmonyOS Flutter port. This directory
adapts the official client's ABI to rd-engine; it does not contain an application.

Name/signature compatibility alone is not feature parity or runtime acceptance.
